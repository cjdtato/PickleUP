Pickleball in the Philippines: the PickleUP community

Pickleball is growing fast across the Philippines. PickleUP brings players, clubs and coaches together in one free web app, so you can spend less time organizing and more time playing.

What you can do on PickleUP

  • Join open play sessions and see who else is coming.
  • Create or join a club with its own chat and announcements.
  • Book lessons with coaches.
  • Complete daily quests and weekly challenges to level up.

New to the game?

Read how to find pickleball games and open play, then open the app and join your first session.
